Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy describes the personal-use Rojbot Gmail connector operated by the owner of the rojjjjjjj1408 GitHub account.

Access and purpose

With the owner’s Google authorisation, Rojbot uses the Gmail API to read account identity, selected message metadata and bodies, and supported PDF attachments. The connector can search and read messages across the connected personal Gmail mailbox for personal organisation, including travel, purchases and events. This includes imported Hotmail messages and sent replies only where those records are stored in Gmail. It does not independently connect to Hotmail. Its Google permission is gmail.readonly; that permission covers reading mailbox data, rather than granting access only to a single sender.

Processing and sharing

The connector runs locally on the owner’s private computer. Email text returned through the connector becomes available to Hermes and may be transmitted to its configured external AI provider for inference. Responses are delivered through the owner’s configured assistant interface, currently WhatsApp. The AI and messaging services process transmitted content under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. This policy does not claim that all processing remains on the local computer.

Google user data is used to provide the personal assistant features requested by the owner. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this application to train a general-purpose AI model. Rojbot’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in private local files. Relevant message content may be retained in Hermes session history. Summaries and source records saved at the owner’s request are retained in the private Obsidian vault, which may sync between the owner’s devices. Private backups may retain these records. PDF extraction uses temporary files that the connector removes after processing.

There is no fixed automatic deletion period for saved notes or session history. The owner controls their retention and deletion; older backup copies may persist until the relevant backups are removed.

Control and revocation

The owner can disconnect Rojbot in their Google Account’s third-party connections settings, stop the connector, and delete locally stored tokens, histories, notes and backups. Revoking Google access prevents further authorised API access but does not delete previously saved records.

This public site

This static information site has no application analytics, advertising, forms or Google sign-in. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may collect technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. No mailbox data or OAuth credentials are published here.

Contact

For questions about this personal project, contact its operator through the GitHub profile. Do not post private messages, credentials or tokens in public issues.
